Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the predictions?

Our AI model analyzes thousands of recent sale transactions, trends across neighborhoods, and key property features to generate highly reliable estimates. While they reflect current market conditions, please use them as guidance—not legal advice or financial guarantees.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We never store personal data such as your email, IP address, or chat content. Your inputs are used only transiently to generate estimates and are promptly discarded—they are never shared with third parties.

Can I rely solely on this tool to decide?

This assistant is designed to help you make informed decisions but should be used in conjunction with professional advice. We highly recommend consulting qualified real estate agents, lawyers, or financial advisors before making final commitments.
